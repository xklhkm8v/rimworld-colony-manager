## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — Colony Manager** is a powerful tool built to help you automate and optimize resource gathering, hunting, forestry, and livestock management in your RimWorld colony. Set resource targets and Colony Manager automatically creates and prioritizes work orders to meet your colony's needs.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — Colony Manager** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. ## 🚀 Features

| **Feature** | **Description** |
|:---|:---|
| **Resource Targets** | Set minimum stock levels and auto-create jobs to meet them |
| **Hunting Manager** | Automatically manage hunting quotas to maintain meat and leather supply |
| **Forestry Manager** | Balance tree cutting and replanting to maintain sustainable wood supply |
| **Livestock Manager** | Automate animal slaughter schedules and taming operations |
